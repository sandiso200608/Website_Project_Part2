Our History

Ubuntu Fresh Market is a fictional South African grocery retailer established in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, in 2022. The business focuses on fresh fruit, vegetables, household groceries and locally sourced products.

Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh, affordable and locally sourced groceries while supporting local farmers and serving the community with quality products.

Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted community grocery brand with a strong digital presence across South Africa.

Why Ubuntu?

The website is designed around convenience, affordability and community. Customers can quickly browse products, check specials and send an enquiry from a phone, tablet or desktop computer.
